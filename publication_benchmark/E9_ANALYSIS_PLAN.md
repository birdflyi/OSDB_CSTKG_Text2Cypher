# E9 evidence and complete-cost accounting

Use immutable scored records and raw calls. Charge unique new API attempts via the shared continuation budget slots, including the connectivity probe, transport failures and all repairs. Copied exact cached records are not a second charged call. Do not sum S0/S1 or G0/G1 paired views as independent usage. Unknown provider usage is not free; do not report a total monetary cost without verified prices/billing.

Report graph-answerable completion, boundary decisions, useful coverage, wrong returned answers, refusal and infrastructure outcomes separately. Show paired outcomes, per-project and semantic-goal strata, and all-attempt vs available-response interpretations. Only compute goal-block uncertainty intervals after formal goal IDs and inclusion are frozen. Synthetic rewrites/role settings do not become independent tasks or human users. Avoid population claims from a convenience workload and four same-source projects.

Record prompt/completion/total tokens, repair overhead, failed-attempt cost and DB work. Model dispatch sums are not actual batch wall time; EXPLAIN/execution times and queue wall time need separate labels. This runner measures query/result generation, not full natural-language conversation, complete RAG pipelines, human usability, or service construction/maintenance.

If lower cost becomes a proposed contribution, compare a compact strong direct-query prompt as well as the equal-information diagnostic prompt. Giving G unnecessary service contracts can inflate its input. Consider deployment/maintenance effort and avoid attributing every gain of protocol/capability expansion to a novel mapping mechanism.

Closing gate: stable useful benefit over strong direct generation at defensible coverage, or a validated actionable boundary analysis beyond the known examples. If neither appears, report that fact and provide a clearly limited first manuscript/research assessment; do not fabricate a positive contribution to force submission. Keep the agreed five-stage scope; extra models/projects/user studies require a concrete claim-based need rather than perfectionism.
